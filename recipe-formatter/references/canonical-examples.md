# Canonical examples

Use these examples to resolve structural questions. Follow `format-spec.md` if an input or example conflicts with a rule.

## Nested components and alternate methods

```text
pasta bolognese

ingredients

proteins:
- ground beef | ground pork - 1 lb

main seasoning:
- [piperaceae] black pepper - ½ tsp
- salt - ½ tsp

deglazing liquid:
- red cooking wine - ½ cup

diced vegetables:
- [allium] diced onions - ½ cup
- [apiaceae] diced carrots - ½ cup

reduced tomato puree:
- [solanum] tomato puree - 2 cups
- baking soda - ¼ tsp

sauce base:
- oil - 2 tbsp
- diced vegetables
- [allium] garlic paste - 1 tbsp
- [solanum] tomato paste - 2 tbsp

assembling:
- cooked pasta - 1 lb
- pasta water - ¼–½ cup

method

reduced tomato puree:
- simmer tomato puree over medium-low heat until slightly thickened
- add baking soda carefully and stir until the foaming settles
- cook until the acidity softens
- target: thicker, mellow tomato base

sauce base:
- heat oil in a wide pan over medium heat
- add diced onions and carrots
- cook until softened
- add garlic paste and cook for 30–60 seconds
- add ground meat and main seasoning
- spread meat evenly and cook until browned
- break apart and continue cooking until no longer pink
- add tomato paste and fry until darker red
- target: savory browned meat with softened vegetables

deglaze:
- add red wine
- scrape the browned bits from the pan
- simmer until the wine reduces
- target: concentrated savory base

stovetop:
- add reduced tomato puree to the pan
- simmer uncovered over medium-low heat until the sauce thickens, about 30–60 min
- stir occasionally
- adjust seasoning if needed
- target: tender meat and vegetables in a rich sauce thick enough to coat pasta

slow cooker:
- transfer the browned meat mixture and reduced tomato puree to a slow cooker
- stir well and cover
- cook until the flavors blend, low: 4–6 hr | high: 2–3 hr
- target: tender meat and vegetables in a rich sauce thick enough to coat pasta

assembling:
- add cooked pasta
- add pasta water a little at a time
- toss until the sauce coats the pasta evenly
- simmer for 1–2 min
- rest briefly before serving

target result:
- browned savory ground meat
- mellow tomato flavor with reduced acidity
- soft onions and carrots
- rich red sauce that clings to pasta
- balanced salt, black pepper, wine depth, and tomato sweetness
```

## Multilingual aliases and reusable components

```text
egusi stew - melon seed stew - sauce de graines de courge

ingredients

protein options:
- meat | chicken | turkey | cow skin | smoked fish | fresh fish | shrimp

main seasoning:
- seasoning cube - ½ tbsp
- salt - ½ tsp

extra seasoning:
- ground crayfish - ½ tbsp
- ground dried shrimp - ½ tbsp
- [monosodium glutamate] msg - ½ tsp

ground melon seed:
- melon seeds - 1 cup
- ground crayfish - ½ tbsp
- ground dried shrimp - ½ tbsp

egusi paste:
- ground melon seed - 1 part
- broth - 1 part

pepper blend:
- [capsicum] red bell peppers - 2
- [capsicum] habanero pepper - 1
- [allium] garlic | [zingiberaceae] ginger - 1
- [allium] onions - ⅓ cup

stew base:
- palm oil - ⅓ cup
- [allium] diced onions - ⅓ cup
- water | broth - ⅓ cup

garnish:
- [allium] sliced onions
- [capsicum] scotch bonnet | habanero peppers
- baby spinach

method

pepper blend:
- blend bell peppers, habanero, garlic or ginger, and onions until smooth
- optionally keep the blend slightly textured

egusi paste:
- mix ground melon seed with broth
- stir until smooth and thick
- target: thick pancake-batter consistency

stew base:
- heat palm oil over medium heat
- add diced onions
- sauté until fragrant and lightly softened
- add pepper blend
- cook over medium heat until the mixture thickens and the palm oil begins to separate slightly
- add main seasoning and extra seasoning
- stir thoroughly

proteins:
- add tougher proteins first with broth
- simmer until partially tender
- add smoked fish and fresh fish
- add shrimp last to prevent overcooking

egusi:
- reduce heat to medium-low
- gently pour or scoop egusi paste into the stew
- allow it to set briefly before stirring
- stir lightly to form soft curds or leave larger clusters if preferred

simmer:
- cook uncovered over medium-low heat
- stir occasionally
- allow the stew to thicken and the egusi to cook fully
- adjust salt if needed

finish:
- add spinach and allow it to wilt
- garnish with sliced onions and whole peppers
- rest for 5 min before serving

target result:
- rich orange palm-oil base
- soft egusi curds suspended in the stew
- savory seafood aroma with deep umami
- balanced heat with nutty melon flavor
- thick, hearty stew consistency
```
